ADDITIONAL VIEWS AND ORIGINAL SKIES
Occupied incubator views use the original machine assets. Wall banners, stone braziers and the circular dais
are flattened renderings of the supplied Hatchery scene; its contributor
notices below continue to apply. These exports do not grant separate rights
to source textures or models. Lit braziers use the kit's native flame artwork.
Day, twilight and night window backgrounds are flattened stills of Pocket
Dragons' original procedural sky artwork. No renderer code is supplied.
The source-scene mountain photograph is not included as a standalone asset.
